When dental problems happen

Don't let dental pain take over your day.

Dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly. A sudden toothache, damaged tooth or painful swelling can make everyday activities difficult and leave you unsure about what to do next.

Night & Day Emergency Dentist helps patients find emergency dental care through its network of clinics. Explore the treatments available and find a location to discuss your symptoms and appointment options.

Find a Clinic Near You

Not sure whether you need emergency dental care?

If you have severe dental pain, swelling, a broken tooth or another urgent dental concern, contact a dental clinic for advice about the appropriate next step.

If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, rapidly spreading swelling, or another potentially life-threatening emergency, seek immediate emergency medical help.

Severe toothache, dental swelling, a suspected abscess, a broken tooth, a lost filling or crown, and certain dental injuries may require urgent dental assessment. A dentist can advise on the appropriate care for your symptoms.

Same-day appointments may be available depending on the clinic, its opening hours and appointment capacity. Contact your selected location to confirm current availability.

Night & Day's website states that patients can access its emergency dental service without registering with a regular dentist first. Confirm the requirements with the clinic you plan to attend.

Contact a dentist promptly if you suspect a dental abscess. Do not attempt to drain it yourself. If swelling makes it difficult to breathe or swallow, or you feel seriously unwell, seek emergency medical help immediately.

Clinic opening hours and appointment availability can vary by location. Do not assume every clinic is open 24 hours. Check the details for your chosen clinic before travelling.

Contact a dental clinic for advice and assessment. If you can safely locate the broken fragment, keep it clean and take it with you. Avoid chewing on the damaged tooth until you have received professional advice.
