Night & Day Emergency Dentist Website Redesign
1. Website Overview
Night & Day Emergency Dentist is a UK-wide dental care network providing emergency dental appointments and selected general and cosmetic treatments across 19 clinic locations.

The redesigned homepage focuses on making emergency dental care easier to find and access. It targets patients experiencing sudden toothache, dental injuries, swelling, broken teeth and other urgent dental problems, including people who are not currently registered with a dentist.

The design direction prioritises clear service communication, accessible clinic discovery, patient reassurance and a straightforward appointment journey.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Emergency Dental Service Presentation
- Reorganised the service presentation to give greater visibility to emergency dental treatments.
- Improved the distinction between urgent services, including tooth extractions, root canal treatment, abscess treatment and broken tooth repairs, and general or cosmetic dentistry.
- Made the core emergency dental offering easier for visitors to understand.
- Addresses the previous challenge of communicating the breadth and priority of emergency services clearly.
2. Improved Clinic Discovery and Location Accessibility
- Strengthened the presentation of the 19 clinic locations across the UK.
- Improved the organisation of location information to help visitors identify relevant clinics.
- Gave greater importance to finding local dental care within the homepage structure.
- Addresses the difficulty of connecting visitors with the appropriate clinic and its local appointment information.
3. Stronger Patient Reassurance and Appointment Journey
- Refined the homepage messaging to communicate accessible emergency dental care more clearly.
- Improved the presentation of appointment-related information and patient contact pathways.
- Emphasised that patients do not need to be existing registered patients to seek care.
- Addresses uncertainty around accessing urgent treatment and finding the appropriate next step.
3. Design Improvements
The redesigned homepage introduces a more structured and patient-focused presentation.

Key improvements include:
- Content hierarchy: Clearer organisation of emergency services, supporting treatments and clinic information.
- Visual presentation: A more consistent and professional layout aligned with the healthcare industry.
- Typography: Improved readability and clearer separation between headings and supporting content.
- Layout: Better organisation of homepage sections to support service discovery and clinic navigation.
- User experience: A more straightforward journey from identifying a dental problem to finding a relevant clinic.
- Patient-focused messaging: Greater emphasis on accessibility, reassurance and urgent dental care.
